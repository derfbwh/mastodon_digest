I've just released my first ever MacOS app! It's something I've been needing for a while and decided to finally build it.

I hope others find it useful as well. If you end up purchasing it, please do let me know as I'd love to hear any and all feedback that you might have!

TopStart is live.

For a while now I've been building a small Mac app quietly, and today it's done: https://topstart.app is live, TopStart is downloadable, and you can buy it. So here's what it is, what it does, and how to get it.
What Is It

TopStart is a menu-bar launcher for Apple Shortcuts. The Shortcuts app is powerful, but reaching the one shortcut you run constantly means opening the app, hunting through a list, waiting for it to load. I got tired of that loop, so I built a tiny thing that lives in your menu bar and keeps your shortcuts one click, or one hotkey, away.

What It Does

Pin the shortcuts you actually use to the menu bar. Click one and it runs. No Dock icon, no window, no waiting for Shortcuts.

Hotkeys. Give any pinned shortcut its own key combination, and set one global quick-open hotkey so you can reach all of them from anywhere, even when the menu bar is hidden.

Type to jump. A Spotlight-style palette (default Option+Command+T) filters your shortcuts as you type. Arrow keys to pick, Enter to run, Esc to dismiss.
Groups. Keep Work, Personal, and custom sets in their own sections, so the right shortcuts are always a glance away.

Start at login. TopStart can launch with your Mac and auto-run whichever shortcuts you choose, spaced out so nothing hammers the system at once.
It's small, it's quiet, and it gets out of your way. That was the whole brief.

Privacy was non-negotiable. Your shortcuts and settings stay on your Mac in a local settings file. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no data collection, no cloud sync. The only time TopStart talks to the internet is a one-time check against Lemon Squeezy when you activate your license key, and it sends just the key.

Price And Trial

TopStart is a one-time purchase. $4.99, forever. No subscription, no upsell. Your license key is emailed to you on purchase, you paste it in once, and it's unlocked permanently, including all future updates.

You don't have to take my word for it first. There's a free trial: download it from https://topstart.app and it runs with every feature for 7 days. Like it? Buy a key and keep it. Don't? Nothing lost.

Where To Get It

Everything lives at https://topstart.app. Grab the free trial, read the details, and hit Buy when you're ready.
This is an indie Mac app I built and shipped end to end, and getting to the point where someone can actually download it and pay for it is a genuinely good feeling. If you try it, I'd love to know what you think.

:boosts_ok_gay: Little PSA for mastodon users

If anyone wants to know what format to upload videos into, that won't take multiple minutes to process (and which will be the format that mastodon re-encodes to anyways), then use the following ffmpeg command;

ffmpeg -i input.mov -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -movflags +faststart output.mp4

Or;

  • MP4
  • Video Codec h264
  • Audio Codec AAC
  • Pixel Format yuv420p

Anything else gets re-encoded, including .mov and .webm, to this.
